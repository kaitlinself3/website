Personal Statement

I’m a 16-year-old graphic designer with two years of experience in design and almost a year specializing in sports graphics. I run my own brand, SelfMadeGraphiz, where I create custom designs like commitment graphics, personalized edits, team schedules, and game-day visuals. My main goal is to make clean, high-quality work that matches what my clients want. I love helping athletes and teams stand out by giving them a professional look that people notice.Besides my design work, I’ve spent the past year learning how to code with JavaScript. I have mastered core concepts like variables, loops, functions, and arrays, while also exploring how to manipulate colors, shapes, and complex digital patterns in real time. This has helped me bring my creativity into the digital world in a new way. I’ve worked on many different projects, from building interactive websites to making my own games and much more. Mixing graphic design with coding sets me apart. I’m always looking for new ways to grow and take on bigger projects that mix my love for sports design with my coding skills. I’m excited to keep learning and see where my design and coding journey goes next. Let's connect to take your brand's visuals to the next level.
